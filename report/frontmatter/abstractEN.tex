This project aims to develop the web application "Dino" as a centralized tourism information platform in Khon Kaen province to address the problem of fragmented and incomplete tourism data. The project integrates artificial intelligence (AI) technologies via Large Language Models (LLMs) combined with Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architecture to develop two main systems: (1) an Adaptive Trip Planning system that generates itineraries based on constraints regarding budget, time, and personal interests, with plan quality automatically verified through an LLM-as-a-Judge mechanism, and (2) a real-time Chatbot system for interactive communication and tourism recommendations. The system utilizes a database of attractions and restaurants retrieved from the Google Places API within Khon Kaen province.

The results show that the backend, frontend, and AI service operate together end-to-end and have been deployed to production at dinokhonkaen.app, including the streaming chatbot and the LLM-based trip planner, together with user account management, the QR-based points collection and redemption system, and the administrative back office. A prompt comparison experiment on the trip planner with the production model (96 runs) found that all generated plans passed the place-validity, required-place, and commonsense checks. A satisfaction questionnaire answered by \surveyN{} users gave an overall mean of \surveyOverallMean{} out of 5 (the \surveyOverallLevelEN{} satisfaction level), with the chatbot and AI trip planning dimensions scoring lower than the others, particularly on response speed. These results show that the application can bring Khon Kaen's tourism information together in one place and generate personalized itineraries in practice, providing a basis for supporting local entrepreneurs and the tourism economy of Khon Kaen province.
